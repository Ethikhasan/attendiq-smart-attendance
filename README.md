# AttendIQ – Smart Attendance Management System

> **Attendance, Reimagined.**

AttendIQ is a smart attendance management platform that helps students and faculty manage, track, and understand attendance efficiently.

## Features

### Student

* View overall and subject-wise attendance
* Attendance history and calendar
* Attendance percentage and trends
* Attendance streak tracking
* Explainable attendance risk
* What-if attendance simulator
* Smart attendance alerts
* Students cannot modify official attendance records

### Faculty

* Create attendance sessions
* Select class and subject
* Mark Present/Absent
* Mark all Present/Absent
* Search and filter students
* Review previous attendance
* Correct attendance with a reason
* View attendance analytics
* Identify students at risk

## Key Innovations

### Explainable Risk

Shows why a student is at risk instead of displaying only an attendance percentage.

### What-If Simulator

Shows how attendance changes if a student attends upcoming classes.

### Attendance Heatmap

Visualizes attendance patterns by date and subject.

### Attendance Streaks

Tracks current and longest attendance streaks.

### Correction Audit

Attendance corrections record the previous status, new status, reason, and timestamp.
